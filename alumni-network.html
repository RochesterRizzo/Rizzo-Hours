How This Works

This is a student-facing alumni directory. The goal is not networking theater. The goal is to help students learn what real paths look like after Rochester economics and to connect serious students with alumni who are willing to be generous with their time.

Student Rules

Do not spam

Write to one or two relevant people with a specific reason. Do not mass email alumni.

Do your homework first

Know who they are, what they do, and why their path is relevant before asking for time.

Ask for advice, not extraction

Do not ask strangers to “get you a job.” Ask thoughtful questions and respect their time.
