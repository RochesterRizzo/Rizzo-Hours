Braddock Bay Raptor Research — Spring 2027 Internships

Spring 2027 · Type: Internship / Field Research / Education · Rochester area

This one is especially worth your attention. I work closely with Braddock Bay Raptor Research, and former students have worked with BBRR as hawk banders and in other capacities.

BBRR is offering two internship tracks for spring 2027. The Raptor Research Internship gives students hands-on experience with the spring hawkwatch, raptor banding, owl-roost surveys, field data collection, raptor identification, and public interpretation. The Raptor Education Internship focuses on caring for and working with non-releasable education raptors, handling and training methods, outreach programs, field trips, and Bird of Prey Days.

The research internship runs roughly March through mid-May and expects at least two days per week, four hours per day. The education internship runs February through May at roughly 4–6 hours per week. Start and end dates are flexible. Own transportation is required.

Application review begins December 1, 2026 and continues on a rolling basis. To apply, send a letter of interest stating which internship you are applying for, a résumé, and references to Daena Ford at information@bbrr.org.

Federal Trade Commission — Bureau of Economics Statisticians

Washington, D.C. · Starts as late as Summer 2027 · One-year term, renewable up to four years

Application deadline: October 24, 2026. Apply through USAJOBS.

Shared by Professor Paulo Barelli for students with strong data skills. The FTC is recruiting graduating seniors and master’s students with coursework in economics, statistics, data science, or computer science.

The work: Work alongside PhD economists on economic and statistical analysis for antitrust and consumer protection investigations and litigation, academic research, and reports on how markets function.

  • GS-07: Strong interest in applied microeconomics; proficiency in Stata, R, or Python; enthusiasm for analyzing varied datasets and working with economists on research.
  • GS-09: Strong applied microeconomics, programming, and data-analysis skills; proficiency in tools such as Stata, R, Python, SQL, or C++; experience with regression, model evaluation, machine learning, or natural language processing. Work may involve terabyte-scale datasets.

Eligibility: The FTC recruiting page specifies U.S. citizenship or nationality and graduation before starting. Check each USAJOBS announcement for the required coursework, education, and experience at that grade.

Apply to both grades if seeking GS-09. The FTC strongly recommends also submitting a separate GS-07 application. Applying only to GS-09 does not put you into consideration for GS-07.

Apply: Statistician GS-07 Apply: Statistician GS-09 Life as an FTC Statistician
Application tips from the FTC

Résumé — two pages or less. Highlight relevant coursework; economics, econometrics, and statistics knowledge; programming; data cleaning and preparation; econometric or statistical analysis; and results presented in graphs, tables, papers, or presentations. The recruiter strongly recommends including hours worked per week for each position and your GPA.

Federal résumé requirements

Cover letter: Explain your interest in economics and the FTC. Give concrete examples of statistics, econometrics, programming, and economic research from coursework, projects, employment, or presentations. Include public-policy interests or experience, especially antitrust or consumer protection, if relevant; prior experience in those topics is not required.

Questions: Aidan Wiemer, Statistician, Antitrust Division II · (202) 651-1866.

TFAS Academic Internship Program — Summer 2027

June 2–July 31, 2027 · Washington, D.C. · Internship / Summer Program

The Fund for American Studies offers eight weeks combining a guaranteed internship, credit-bearing George Mason University courses, and furnished housing, plus professional development, site briefings, and networking.

Economics students should look at the Public Policy & Economics track. Other tracks cover international affairs, journalism and communications, and business and government relations.

Early application deadline: November 5, 2026.

Scholarships: TFAS advertises more than 80 full scholarships and hundreds of partial awards. Program fees apply; submit your scholarship request with your admissions application.

Eligibility: At least 18 years old, a high-school graduate, and at least one completed semester of undergraduate study at an accredited college or university.

Other deadlines: January 8, 2027 (priority); February 8, 2027 (final). Applicants needing a J-1 visa have an October 8, 2026 deadline; see TFAS admissions for international eligibility.

Paychex Data Research Opportunity — September 9

Past opportunity · Wednesday, September 9, 2026 · Type: Research / Data · Rochester

Professor Ronni Pavan is working with Paychex on a new confidential dataset containing county-level measures of firms’ hiring, separations, employment, and compensation across the United States. The initial project will examine the effects of the ACA expansion, but the data potentially open the door to many other research questions as well.

Interested students were invited to meet representatives from Paychex on Wednesday, September 9:

  • Around 12:30 p.m. — Harkness 210
  • 2:00 p.m. — Dewey 2110E

This was an unusual opportunity for students interested in empirical economics, independent research, or potentially developing a future thesis idea.

Think of this as an example of the kinds of research opportunities that can appear during the semester: working with a remarkable real-world dataset, seeing what questions emerge, and learning what serious empirical research looks like.

The data were to be stored securely on BlueHive, with access potentially available to interested students willing to sign the necessary confidentiality agreements.
