NOW CHANGE SOMETHING

Do not stop at one answer. Move assumptions, parameters, scenarios, populations, technologies, time horizons, or other meaningful inputs and show what happens.

Variation 1: change one assumption while holding the rest fixed.
Variation 2: change a second assumption or compare a competing scenario on the same scale.

WHAT ARE WE ACTUALLY LOOKING AT?

Explain the mechanism and scale. What caused the result to move? What is large, small, surprising, or sensitive? What does the graph not establish?

Then provide sources, units, assumptions, calculations/code, and a brief account of what AI helped you find, build, check, or revise.

WHY THIS COUNTS AS A WORKING TEST EXHIBIT

It has a public URL, a stable page, a clear question, a visual, two changed assumptions, interpretation, and an open-the-hood section. A real student exhibit should replace the synthetic demonstration with serious environmental-economics evidence and analysis.
