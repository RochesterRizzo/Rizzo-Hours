STUDENT FAQ

Claiming a SHOW ME

How many do I choose?

Two. The system will not let you hold more than two active reservations.

Can two students claim the same numbered topic?

No. Numbered topics are first-come, first-served. The claim is locked when the first valid submission reaches the database.

What is “Create a Topic on My Own”?

It is a permanent option available to everyone. You may use it for one of your two investigations. It never becomes unavailable just because another student used it.

Can both of my investigations be custom topics?

No. Create a Topic on My Own can be used for only one of your two SHOW MEs.

I changed my mind.

Use your private management link to release a reservation. A numbered topic becomes available to the class again immediately.

I lost my management link.

Contact the instructional staff. Do not create a second identity with another email address.

I clicked claim at the same time as someone else.

The database decides. The first completed valid claim wins; the other student will be returned to the topic list.

Is claiming the topic the same as submitting the finished exhibit?

No. This tool only reserves your two investigations. Finished SHOW ME exhibits will be submitted to the Museum separately.
