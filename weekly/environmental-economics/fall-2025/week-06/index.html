Chart of the Week

“We begin by quantifying the sheer scale of what the US has already constructed. In early September, the US Department of Transportation’s Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration released its annual report on the total mileage of natural gas pipelines. According to the document, more than 110,000 miles of gathering pipelines, 300,000 miles of transmission pipelines, and 2.37 million miles of distribution pipelines crisscross the nation. As their names imply, gathering pipelines bring gas from wellheads to processing facilities, transmission pipelines move molecules across long distances in large volumes, and distribution pipelines deliver gas to the homes and businesses that ultimately burn the fuel.”
