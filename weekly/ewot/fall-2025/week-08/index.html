You can start to read the materials in Section I, Part E. We are starting on our Basic Principles this Friday, and will continue in that section next week.

Exam information

Exam: We do not post solutions or grade distributions to the exams. However look for the TA announcements as they will be hosting at least two session(s) to go over it with you in person next week.

Our exam will be graded and scores will be uploaded to Blackboard by the end of the weekend. Your recitation TA will have your blue book and will make arrangements with you to have them returned.

Please read the syllabus regarding course grading and exam policies.

Remember this is your first EWOT exam, and we aim to use it as a teaching tool not merely an evaluation tool. So when you see the exam scores, we want you to see how your raw score really stacks up – that way you can secure feedback on what you may need to continue working on. But once again, I urge you to consider, that we may not weight the exam as heavily (or much) if the class continues to work hard, and you continue to show improvement – especially as the final exam rolls around.

The Immigration Prize: “Mr. Yaghi, a Berkeley professor, came to the U.S. as a teenager in a refugee family and had limited knowledge of English. He studied at a community college in Troy, N.Y., earned his Ph.D. at the University of Illinois, and had faculty positions at Arizona State, Michigan and UCLA before joining Cal. Only in America, as some of us still like to say.

Professor Yaghi’s path speaks to the varieties of the U.S. immigrant experience. You never know who or how the poorest refugee or migrant might blossom into a world-class scientist or entrepreneur. This is talent the U.S. needs, and immigration is a force multiplier for American innovation.

Stuart Anderson at the National Foundation for American Policy has looked at the three science Nobels awarded since 2000, and immigrants make up 40% of the U.S. winners. As the nearby chart shows, it’s 45% for physics, 43% for chemistry and 32% for medicine.”
